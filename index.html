ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Yuzhou Chen. You can also call me Roki.

I study transportation systems through simulation, data-driven modeling, and optimization. My research interests include human–AI interaction in semi-autonomous driving, driver behavior, and the design of more efficient and sustainable mobility systems.

My academic background includes graduate study in Civil Engineering–Transportation at the University of Michigan and a bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from Zhejiang University. Working with Prof. Neda Masoud, I have developed a CARLA-based virtual reality driving platform that integrates eye-tracking and real-time human-in-the-loop interaction.

Research interests Driving simulation · Human–AI interaction · Transportation modeling · Network optimization
